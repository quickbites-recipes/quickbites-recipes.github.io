Egg Fried Rice

This is the recipe that saved me from ordering takeout more times than I can count. It's faster than delivery, cheaper, and honestly better once you get the technique right.

Ingredients

Serves 2.

  • Cooked rice, cold (day-old works best)2.5 cups
  • Eggs2
  • Carrot, finely diced1/2 cup
  • Peas1/4 cup
  • Spring onion, chopped3 tbsp
  • Garlic, chopped2 cloves
  • Oil3 tbsp
  • Soy sauce2 tbsp
  • Salt and white or black pepperto taste

Method

  1. Scramble the eggs. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a wide pan or wok over high heat. Beat the eggs lightly and pour them in, scrambling quickly into small pieces. Remove and set aside.
  2. Cook the vegetables. Add the remaining oil to the same pan. Add the garlic, carrot, and peas, and stir-fry for 2-3 minutes until just tender.
  3. Add the rice. Break up any clumps in the cold rice and add it to the pan. Toss and press it against the hot pan for a couple of minutes to heat through and get slightly crisp in spots.
  4. Combine and finish. Return the scrambled eggs to the pan along with the soy sauce, salt, and pepper. Toss everything together for another minute. Finish with spring onion and serve hot.

Tip: Cook the eggs separately and add them back at the end — this keeps them in distinct, fluffy pieces instead of turning rubbery from overcooking with the rice.

Why this works

High heat and cold, day-old rice are both non-negotiable here. High heat sears rather than steams the rice, and dry, cold rice grains stay separate instead of clumping into a sticky mass.
