ONE POT · 20 MINUTES

Weeknight Chicken Karahi

Karahi usually means a big weekend production — but this version strips it down to what actually matters: tomatoes, chicken, and a handful of spices cooked hot and fast in one pot. It won't taste like it took twenty minutes.

Ingredients

Serves 3-4.

  • Chicken, boneless, cut into pieces500g
  • Tomatoes, chopped4 medium
  • Ginger-garlic paste1.5 tbsp
  • Green chilies, slit3-4
  • Cooking oil4 tbsp
  • Cumin seeds1 tsp
  • Red chili powder1 tsp
  • Saltto taste
  • Fresh coriander, choppedhandful

Method

  1. Sear the chicken. Heat the oil in a wide pan or wok over high heat. Add the cumin seeds, then the chicken pieces. Sear for 3-4 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the outside turns opaque.
  2. Add aromatics. Stir in the ginger-garlic paste and cook for a minute until the raw smell fades.
  3. Cook the tomatoes down. Add the chopped tomatoes, chili powder, and salt. Cook on high heat, mashing the tomatoes with your spoon as they soften, until the mixture thickens into a coating sauce — about 8-10 minutes.
  4. Finish. Add the green chilies, cover, and cook for another 3-4 minutes until the chicken is fully cooked through. Uncover and let any extra liquid cook off on high heat for the last minute. Top with fresh coriander and serve with roti or rice.

Tip: Cooking the tomatoes on high heat without a lid is what gives karahi its slightly charred, concentrated flavor — resist the urge to add water to speed things up.

Why this works

Traditional karahi relies on cooking tomatoes down until the oil separates, which usually takes a while. Using high heat and boneless chicken (which cooks faster than bone-in) gets you most of that same depth of flavor in a fraction of the time.
