One-Pot Vegetable Pulao

Pulao often gets treated as a special-occasion dish, but the everyday version — just rice, vegetables, and whole spices in one pot — is simple enough for a Tuesday night.

Ingredients

Serves 3.

  • Basmati rice, soaked 15 minutes1.5 cups
  • Mixed vegetables (peas, carrot, beans)1.5 cups
  • Onion, sliced1 medium
  • Whole cumin seeds1 tsp
  • Cinnamon stick1 small
  • Cloves3
  • Oil or ghee3 tbsp
  • Water2.5 cups
  • Saltto taste

Method

  1. Fry the whole spices. Heat the oil in a pot over medium heat. Add the cumin seeds, cinnamon, and cloves, and let them sizzle for about 20 seconds until fragrant.
  2. Cook the onions. Add the sliced onion and cook, stirring occasionally, for 3-4 minutes until softened and light golden.
  3. Add vegetables and rice. Stir in the mixed vegetables and cook for 2 minutes. Drain the soaked rice and add it to the pot, stirring gently for a minute to coat the grains without breaking them.
  4. Cook the rice. Add the water and salt, and bring to a boil. Once boiling, cover, reduce the heat to low, and cook undisturbed for 12-15 minutes. Turn off the heat and let it rest, covered, for 5 minutes before fluffing gently with a fork.

Tip: Soaking the rice for even 15 minutes before cooking helps each grain cook evenly and stay separate instead of turning sticky.

Why this works

Frying the whole spices in oil before anything else releases their essential oils, which is what gives pulao its fragrance. Adding them straight into water instead would leave most of that flavor behind.
