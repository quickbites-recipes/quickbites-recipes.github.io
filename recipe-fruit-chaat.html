Spiced Fruit Chaat

A little chaat masala and lemon juice can turn plain chopped fruit into something you actually crave — sweet, tangy, and a little spicy all at once.

Ingredients

Serves 2.

  • Apple, diced1
  • Banana, sliced1
  • Pomegranate seeds1/4 cup
  • Guava or pear, diced1
  • Chaat masala1 tsp
  • Black salt1/4 tsp
  • Lemon1/2
  • Fresh mint leaves, chopped (optional)a few

Method

  1. Chop the fruit. Cut all the fruit into roughly similar-sized bite pieces so each spoonful gets a mix of textures.
  2. Combine. Add all the fruit to a large bowl along with the pomegranate seeds.
  3. Season. Sprinkle the chaat masala and black salt over the fruit, then squeeze the lemon juice on top.
  4. Toss and serve. Toss gently so the fruit doesn't get mashed. Add mint leaves if using, and serve immediately for the best texture.

Tip: Add banana last and toss it in gently — it's the fruit most likely to turn mushy if mixed too early or too roughly.

Why this works

Chaat masala's tangy, slightly sulfurous flavor comes from dried mango powder and black salt, both of which are built to cut through sweetness rather than mask it — that's why it works on fruit as well as it does on savory snacks.
