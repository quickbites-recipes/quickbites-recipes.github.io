Aloo Paratha

A good aloo paratha is one of those things that takes a little practice but never takes long once you've got the rhythm down — spiced mashed potato, folded into dough, and cooked until crisp and golden.

Ingredients

Makes 4 parathas.

  • Whole wheat flour2 cups
  • Wateras needed for dough
  • Potatoes, boiled and mashed3 medium
  • Onion, finely chopped2 tbsp
  • Green chili, chopped1
  • Red chili powder1/2 tsp
  • Ajwain (carom seeds)1/4 tsp
  • Saltto taste
  • Ghee or oilfor cooking

Method

  1. Make the dough. Knead the flour with water until you get a soft, smooth dough. Cover and let it rest for 10 minutes.
  2. Prepare the filling. Mix the mashed potatoes with onion, green chili, chili powder, ajwain, and salt until evenly combined. Divide into 4 portions.
  3. Stuff the paratha. Roll a ball of dough into a small circle, place a portion of filling in the center, and gather the edges up and over the filling to seal it completely. Flatten gently and roll out carefully into a larger circle, dusting with flour as needed to prevent tearing.
  4. Cook. Heat a griddle or flat pan over medium heat. Cook the paratha for about a minute, then flip. Spread a little ghee on top, flip again, and spread ghee on the other side. Cook, flipping occasionally, until both sides have golden-brown spots.

Tip: Make sure the mashed potato filling isn't too wet — excess moisture is the main reason parathas tear while rolling.

Why this works

Sealing the filling completely before rolling is the step that prevents it from bursting out. Rolling gently, from the center outward, also keeps the filling evenly distributed instead of pushed to one side.
