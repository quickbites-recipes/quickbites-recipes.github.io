Chicken Shawarma Wrap

You don't need a vertical rotisserie to get shawarma-style flavor at home. A hot pan and the right spice mix get you most of the way there, wrapped up and ready in about 20 minutes.

Ingredients

Makes 2 wraps.

  • Chicken breast or boneless thigh, sliced thin300g
  • Yogurt2 tbsp
  • Garlic paste1 tsp
  • Cumin powder1/2 tsp
  • Paprika or red chili powder1/2 tsp
  • Oil2 tbsp
  • Flatbreads or tortillas2
  • Lettuce or cabbage, shredded1/2 cup
  • Tomato, sliced1
  • Garlic sauce or plain yogurt, for drizzling2-3 tbsp

Method

  1. Marinate the chicken. In a bowl, mix the yogurt, garlic paste, cumin, paprika, and a pinch of salt. Add the sliced chicken and toss to coat. Let it sit for at least 10 minutes while you prep the rest — longer if you have time.
  2. Cook the chicken. Heat the oil in a pan over high heat. Add the chicken in a single layer and cook for 4-5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until browned at the edges and cooked through.
  3. Warm the bread. Briefly warm the flatbreads in a dry pan or directly over a low flame for a few seconds on each side, just until soft and pliable.
  4. Assemble. Lay a flatbread flat, add a layer of lettuce and tomato, top with the cooked chicken, and drizzle with garlic sauce. Fold in the sides and roll tightly into a wrap.

Tip: Slicing the chicken thin and against the grain before marinating helps it cook faster and stay tender rather than chewy.

Why this works

The yogurt in the marinade does double duty — it carries the spices and its mild acidity helps tenderize the chicken, which is part of why traditional shawarma marinades almost always include a dairy or citrus base.
