5-Ingredient Curd Toast

Some mornings you don't have time to stand at a stove, and this is the recipe for exactly that kind of morning. No cooking, no heating a pan — just toast, a good spread, and five minutes of assembly.

Ingredients

Serves 1.

  • Bread slices2
  • Thick curd (yogurt), strained1/2 cup
  • Cucumber, thinly sliced1/2
  • Roasted cumin powder1/4 tsp
  • Salt and black pepperto taste

Method

  1. Toast the bread. Toast both slices until golden and slightly crisp — this keeps the toast from turning soggy once the curd goes on.
  2. Strain the curd. If your curd looks watery, spoon it into a fine sieve for a minute to drain excess water. Thicker curd holds better on toast.
  3. Spread and layer. Spread a generous layer of curd over each toast. Arrange the cucumber slices on top.
  4. Season and serve. Sprinkle with roasted cumin powder, salt, and black pepper. Eat immediately while the toast is still crisp.

Tip: Add a drizzle of honey or a few mint leaves if you want a slightly different flavor each time — the base recipe stays the same.

Why this works

Straining the curd is the one step people skip, and it's the reason store-bought curd toast often turns into mush. A thicker curd sits on the bread instead of soaking into it, so you get texture in every bite.
