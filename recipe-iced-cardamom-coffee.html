Iced Cardamom Coffee

You don't need an espresso machine or a trip to a café for this. A pinch of cardamom turns ordinary instant coffee into something that tastes like it came from a menu.

Ingredients

Serves 1.

  • Instant coffee1.5 tsp
  • Hot water2 tbsp
  • Sugar1-2 tsp
  • Ground cardamom1/4 tsp
  • Cold milk1 cup
  • Ice cubesa handful

Method

  1. Whip the coffee base. In a small bowl, combine the instant coffee, hot water, and sugar. Whisk vigorously with a spoon or small whisk for about 2 minutes until it turns pale, thick, and foamy.
  2. Fill the glass. Fill a tall glass with ice cubes, then pour in the cold milk, leaving room at the top.
  3. Add cardamom. Stir the ground cardamom into the milk so it's evenly distributed rather than sitting in one clump.
  4. Top and serve. Spoon the whipped coffee foam over the top of the milk. Give it one light stir just before drinking, or leave it layered for a nicer look.

Tip: Whisking the coffee with only a small amount of hot water (not a full cup) is what makes it foam up — too much liquid and it stays flat.

Why this works

Cardamom has a natural sweetness and floral note that pairs unusually well with the bitterness of coffee — it's the same principle behind cardamom-spiced chai, just applied to a cold coffee instead.
