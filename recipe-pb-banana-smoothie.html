Peanut Butter Banana Smoothie

When there's no time to sit down and eat, this is the closest thing to a full breakfast you can drink out of a glass on your way out the door.

Ingredients

Serves 1.

  • Ripe banana, frozen if possible1
  • Peanut butter1.5 tbsp
  • Milk1 cup
  • Honey1 tsp (optional)
  • Ice cubes3-4 (skip if using frozen banana)

Method

  1. Prep the banana. Break the banana into a few chunks so it blends more easily.
  2. Blend. Add the banana, peanut butter, milk, honey, and ice to a blender. Blend on high for about 30-40 seconds until completely smooth.
  3. Check consistency. If it's too thick, add a splash more milk and blend again briefly. If it's too thin, add another ice cube or a few extra pieces of frozen banana.
  4. Serve immediately. Pour into a glass and drink right away, before the ice starts to separate.

Tip: Keep a few peeled, chopped bananas in the freezer at all times — a frozen banana gives this smoothie a thick, milkshake-like texture without watering it down with ice.

Why this works

Peanut butter and banana both bring natural creaminess, so this smoothie doesn't need yogurt or protein powder to feel filling — it's dense enough on its own to hold you over for a few hours.
