Besan Chilla

Think of this as a savory pancake made from gram flour instead of wheat — no yeast, no resting time, and it comes together entirely from pantry staples.

Ingredients

Makes 3 chillas.

  • Gram flour (besan)1 cup
  • Water3/4 cup, adjust as needed
  • Onion, finely chopped2 tbsp
  • Tomato, finely chopped2 tbsp
  • Green chili, chopped1
  • Turmeric powder1/4 tsp
  • Carom seeds (ajwain)1/4 tsp
  • Saltto taste
  • Oilfor cooking

Method

  1. Make the batter. In a bowl, whisk the gram flour with water gradually, breaking up any lumps, until you get a smooth, pourable batter similar to pancake batter.
  2. Add the mix-ins. Stir in the onion, tomato, green chili, turmeric, carom seeds, and salt until evenly combined.
  3. Cook the first side. Heat a non-stick pan over medium heat and lightly grease it. Pour a ladleful of batter and spread it into a thin, even circle. Drizzle a little oil around the edges and cook for 2-3 minutes until the base turns golden.
  4. Flip and finish. Flip carefully and cook the other side for another 1-2 minutes until cooked through and lightly browned. Serve hot with chutney or ketchup.

Tip: If the batter seems too thick after the vegetables release some water, thin it with a tablespoon of water at a time until it spreads easily.

Why this works

Gram flour behaves differently from wheat flour — it doesn't need gluten development or resting, so the batter can go from bowl to pan in under two minutes, which is what makes chilla one of the fastest hot breakfasts there is.
