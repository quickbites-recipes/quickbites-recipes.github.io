Mango Lassi

When mangoes are in season, this is the simplest way to enjoy them — blended with curd into a thick, cooling drink that needs nothing more than a blender and five minutes.

Ingredients

Serves 2.

  • Ripe mango, chopped1 large
  • Plain curd (yogurt)1 cup
  • Milk1/2 cup
  • Sugar or honey1-2 tbsp, to taste
  • Cardamom powdera pinch (optional)
  • Ice cubes4-5

Method

  1. Prep the mango. Peel and chop the mango into chunks, discarding the seed.
  2. Blend. Add the mango, curd, milk, sugar, and cardamom (if using) to a blender. Blend until completely smooth.
  3. Add ice. Add the ice cubes and blend again for another 15-20 seconds until the lassi is cold and slightly frothy.
  4. Taste and adjust. Taste and add more sugar if the mango isn't very sweet, or a splash more milk if it's too thick. Pour into glasses and serve immediately.

Tip: The sweetness of the mango varies a lot, so always add sugar gradually and taste as you go rather than following a fixed amount.

Why this works

Blending the curd with milk (rather than curd alone) keeps the lassi pourable instead of overly thick, while still giving it that classic tang that balances the mango's sweetness.
