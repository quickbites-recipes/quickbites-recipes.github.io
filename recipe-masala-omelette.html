Masala Omelette

Eggs are the fastest protein in the kitchen, and a few chopped vegetables and spices turn a plain omelette into something worth waking up for.

Ingredients

Serves 1.

  • Eggs2
  • Onion, finely chopped2 tbsp
  • Tomato, finely chopped2 tbsp
  • Green chili, chopped1
  • Turmeric powder1/4 tsp
  • Oil1 tbsp
  • Salt and black pepperto taste

Method

  1. Whisk the eggs. Crack the eggs into a bowl and whisk well with salt, pepper, and turmeric until fully combined and slightly frothy.
  2. Add the vegetables. Stir the onion, tomato, and green chili directly into the whisked eggs.
  3. Cook. Heat the oil in a non-stick pan over medium heat. Pour in the egg mixture and let it sit undisturbed for about a minute until the edges set.
  4. Fold and finish. Once the base is set but the top is still slightly wet, fold the omelette in half. Cook for another 30-60 seconds on each side until fully cooked through. Serve hot.

Tip: Keep the heat at medium, not high — a fast, high-heat omelette turns rubbery. A gentler heat keeps the eggs soft.

Why this works

Chopping the vegetables small and mixing them into the egg before cooking (rather than sprinkling them on top) means everything cooks evenly in the same short time it takes the egg to set.
