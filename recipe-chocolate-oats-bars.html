No-Bake Chocolate Oats Bars

No oven, no baking powder, no waiting around for something to rise. Just four ingredients melted together, pressed into a pan, and chilled until firm.

Ingredients

Makes 8 bars.

  • Rolled oats2 cups
  • Honey or golden syrup1/2 cup
  • Butter3 tbsp
  • Cocoa powder2 tbsp

Method

  1. Melt the wet ingredients. In a saucepan over low heat, melt the butter with the honey, stirring until combined and slightly bubbling at the edges — about 2 minutes.
  2. Add cocoa. Stir in the cocoa powder until fully dissolved and the mixture looks glossy.
  3. Mix in the oats. Take the pan off the heat and add the oats. Stir thoroughly until every oat is coated in the chocolate mixture.
  4. Press and chill. Tip the mixture into a small lined pan or dish. Press down firmly and evenly with the back of a spoon — the firmer you pack it, the better the bars hold together. Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes before slicing into bars.

Tip: If you're in a hurry, the freezer sets these in about 12 minutes instead of 30 in the fridge.

Why this works

Honey does double duty here — it sweetens the bars and, once cooled, firms up enough to act as the glue holding the oats together. That's why there's no flour or egg needed the way a baked bar would require.
