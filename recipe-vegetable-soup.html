Simple Vegetable Soup

Some nights call for something light rather than heavy. This soup is built from whatever vegetables are sitting in the fridge, simmered until soft, and blended into something warm and simple.

Ingredients

Serves 2-3.

  • Carrot, chopped2 medium
  • Potato, chopped1 medium
  • Onion, chopped1 small
  • Garlic, chopped2 cloves
  • Vegetable or chicken stock (or water)3 cups
  • Butter or oil1 tbsp
  • Salt and black pepperto taste
  • Cream or milk2 tbsp (optional)

Method

  1. Soften the aromatics. Heat the butter in a pot over medium heat. Add the onion and garlic and cook for 2-3 minutes until softened, without browning.
  2. Add the vegetables. Stir in the carrot and potato and cook for another minute.
  3. Simmer. Pour in the stock, add salt, and bring to a boil. Reduce the heat and simmer for 12-15 minutes until the vegetables are completely soft when pierced with a fork.
  4. Blend and finish. Blend the soup until smooth using a hand blender, or carefully in batches in a regular blender. Return to the pot, stir in the cream if using, and adjust salt and pepper before serving.

Tip: If you don't have a blender, just mash the vegetables with a fork or potato masher directly in the pot for a rustic, chunkier soup instead.

Why this works

Potato is the quiet workhorse in this soup — it breaks down when blended and naturally thickens the broth, so there's no need for flour, cornstarch, or extra cream to get a satisfying, velvety texture.
