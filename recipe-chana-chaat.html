SNACK · NO COOK · 10 MINUTES

Chana Chaat

This is the snack that disappears the fastest at any gathering — tangy, spiced chickpeas tossed with onion, tomato, and a hit of lemon, ready in the time it takes to chop a few vegetables.

Ingredients

Serves 2-3.

  • Boiled or canned chickpeas, drained2 cups
  • Onion, finely chopped1/2 medium
  • Tomato, finely chopped1 medium
  • Green chili, chopped1
  • Fresh coriander, chopped2 tbsp
  • Chaat masala1 tsp
  • Red chili powder1/4 tsp
  • Lemon1
  • Saltto taste

Method

  1. Prep the vegetables. Finely chop the onion, tomato, green chili, and coriander while the chickpeas drain.
  2. Combine. Add the chickpeas, onion, tomato, and green chili to a large bowl.
  3. Season. Sprinkle the chaat masala, chili powder, and salt over the top, then squeeze the lemon juice over everything.
  4. Toss and finish. Toss well until evenly coated, top with fresh coriander, and taste for seasoning — add more lemon or salt if needed. Serve right away.

Tip: If making this ahead, keep the lemon and salt separate and add them just before serving — salt draws water out of the vegetables and can make the chaat soggy if it sits too long.

Why this works

Chana chaat relies on contrast — the softness of the chickpeas against the crunch of raw onion, and the tang of lemon cutting through the earthy chaat masala. No cooking is needed because every ingredient is already working with its natural texture.
