Pan-Fried Seekh Kebab

No grill, no charcoal, no skewers — just ground meat, a handful of spices, and a hot pan. These come out just as juicy as the barbecue version, minus the setup.

Ingredients

Makes 8 kebabs.

  • Ground beef or chicken500g
  • Onion, finely grated and squeezed dry1 small
  • Ginger-garlic paste1 tbsp
  • Green chili, finely chopped1-2
  • Roasted cumin powder1 tsp
  • Red chili powder1/2 tsp
  • Fresh coriander, chopped2 tbsp
  • Gram flour (besan), lightly toasted2 tbsp
  • Saltto taste
  • Oilfor cooking

Method

  1. Mix the kebab base. In a bowl, combine the ground meat, grated onion, ginger-garlic paste, green chili, cumin, chili powder, coriander, toasted gram flour, and salt. Mix thoroughly with your hands until well combined and slightly sticky.
  2. Rest the mixture. Cover and refrigerate for 10-15 minutes — this firms up the mixture and makes it easier to shape.
  3. Shape the kebabs. With slightly wet hands, take a portion of the mixture and shape it into an oval or log shape, about the size of your palm.
  4. Cook. Heat a couple tablespoons of oil in a pan over medium heat. Cook the kebabs for 3-4 minutes per side, turning carefully, until browned on the outside and cooked through. Serve with naan and chutney.

Tip: Squeezing the grated onion dry before adding it is essential — too much liquid in the mixture makes the kebabs fall apart while cooking.

Why this works

Toasted gram flour acts as a binder here, the same way breadcrumbs work in a meatball, holding the mixture together without a skewer. Resting the mix in the fridge also firms up the fat, so the kebabs hold their shape better in the pan.
