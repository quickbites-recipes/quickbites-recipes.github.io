15-Minute Garlic Butter Pasta

This is the recipe I make when the fridge is nearly empty but I still want something that feels like an actual meal. It's built entirely from things most kitchens already have — pasta, butter, garlic, a bit of cheese — and it comes together in the same time it takes the pasta to cook.

Ingredients

Serves 2.

  • Spaghetti or any long pasta200g
  • Butter3 tbsp
  • Garlic, finely chopped4 cloves
  • Red chili flakes1/2 tsp
  • Parmesan or any hard cheese, grated1/3 cup
  • Salt and black pepperto taste
  • Pasta water (saved)1/2 cup

Method

  1. Boil the pasta. Salt the water generously and cook the pasta until just short of al dente — it'll finish cooking in the pan. Save half a cup of the starchy water before draining.
  2. Build the sauce. While the pasta boils, melt the butter in a wide pan over medium-low heat. Add the garlic and chili flakes, and cook gently for about a minute until fragrant — don't let the garlic brown or it turns bitter.
  3. Combine. Add the drained pasta straight into the pan along with a splash of the saved pasta water. Toss well so the starch helps the butter cling to every strand.
  4. Finish. Take the pan off the heat, stir in the cheese, and toss again until glossy. Add more pasta water a tablespoon at a time if it looks dry. Season with salt and pepper, and serve immediately.

Tip: If you want to bulk this up, toss in a handful of spinach in step 3, or top with a fried egg — both add substance without adding cooking time.

Why this works

The trick to a good butter pasta isn't a long ingredient list — it's the starchy pasta water. It emulsifies with the butter instead of just floating on top, which is what gives the sauce that glossy, restaurant-like coating instead of a greasy puddle at the bottom of the bowl.
