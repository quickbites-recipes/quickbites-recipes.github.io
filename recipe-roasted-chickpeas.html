Spiced Roasted Chickpeas

This is the snack I make instead of opening a bag of chips. It's crunchy, salty, and a little spicy — and it comes together on the stovetop in the time it takes a kettle to boil.

Ingredients

Serves 2 as a snack.

  • Boiled or canned chickpeas, drained1.5 cups
  • Oil1.5 tbsp
  • Red chili powder1/2 tsp
  • Chaat masala1/2 tsp
  • Saltto taste

Method

  1. Dry the chickpeas. Pat the drained chickpeas dry with a kitchen towel. This step matters more than it sounds — wet chickpeas steam instead of crisping up.
  2. Heat the pan. Heat the oil in a pan over medium-high heat until it's shimmering.
  3. Roast. Add the chickpeas and spread them in a single layer. Let them sit for a minute before stirring, then toss occasionally for 3-4 minutes until the skins start to crisp and turn slightly golden.
  4. Season and serve. Take off the heat, toss with chili powder, chaat masala, and salt while still hot. Eat right away for maximum crunch.

Tip: Don't season the chickpeas before roasting — spices can burn in the hot oil. Always season after they come off the heat.

Why this works

Drying the chickpeas before they hit the oil is the difference between a crisp snack and a soggy one. Any surface moisture turns to steam in the pan, which stops the chickpeas from ever getting properly crunchy.
