10-Minute Garlic Soy Noodles

This is what happens when instant noodles grow up — same speed, but the seasoning packet gets replaced with real garlic, soy sauce, and a couple of vegetables.

Ingredients

Serves 1-2.

  • Noodles (instant or any boiled noodles)1 block / 150g
  • Garlic, finely chopped4 cloves
  • Spring onion, sliced (white and green separated)3 tbsp
  • Oil2 tbsp
  • Soy sauce2 tbsp
  • Vinegar1 tsp
  • Black pepper1/4 tsp

Method

  1. Boil the noodles. Cook the noodles according to package instructions until just tender, then drain and set aside. If using instant noodles, skip the seasoning packet.
  2. Fry the garlic. Heat the oil in a pan over medium-high heat. Add the garlic and the white parts of the spring onion, and stir-fry for about 30 seconds until fragrant but not browned.
  3. Add the noodles. Add the drained noodles to the pan along with the soy sauce and vinegar. Toss well using tongs or two forks so the sauce coats every strand.
  4. Finish. Cook for another minute, tossing occasionally, until everything is hot and well combined. Finish with black pepper and the green parts of the spring onion, then serve immediately.

Tip: Slightly undercook the noodles when boiling — they'll continue cooking for another minute or two once tossed in the hot pan with the sauce.

Why this works

Frying the garlic in oil before adding the noodles builds a flavor base the way a proper stir-fry does, instead of just relying on a seasoning packet dumped in at the end — it's a small step that changes the whole dish.
