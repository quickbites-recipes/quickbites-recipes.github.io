One-Pan Vegetable Stir Fry

This is less a fixed recipe and more a method — once you know the sauce and the technique, you can stir-fry almost any vegetables sitting in your fridge and get a solid result.

Ingredients

Serves 2.

  • Mixed vegetables (carrot, capsicum, cabbage, beans), sliced thin3 cups
  • Garlic, chopped3 cloves
  • Oil2 tbsp
  • Soy sauce2 tbsp
  • Vinegar1 tsp
  • Sugar1/2 tsp
  • Black pepper1/4 tsp

Method

  1. Prep everything first. Stir-frying moves fast, so slice all the vegetables and mix the soy sauce, vinegar, and sugar together in a small bowl before you turn on the heat.
  2. Heat the pan hot. Use a wide pan or wok and heat the oil over high heat until it's just starting to shimmer.
  3. Cook the vegetables in order. Add the garlic first and stir for 10 seconds, then add the harder vegetables (carrot, beans) and stir-fry for 2 minutes. Add the softer ones (capsicum, cabbage) and cook for another 2 minutes, keeping everything moving so it sears rather than steams.
  4. Sauce and finish. Pour the sauce mixture over the vegetables and toss for 30-45 seconds until everything is coated and glossy. Finish with black pepper and serve immediately while the vegetables are still crisp.

Tip: Don't crowd the pan — if you're doubling the recipe, cook it in two batches. Too many vegetables at once drops the pan's temperature and they end up steaming instead of getting that slight char.

Why this works

Cutting vegetables thin and adding them in order of how long they take to cook (hard ones first, soft ones last) means everything finishes at the same time, staying crisp instead of turning to mush.
