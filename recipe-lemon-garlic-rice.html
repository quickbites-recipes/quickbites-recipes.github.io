Lemon Garlic Rice Bowl

Leftover rice sitting in the fridge is an opportunity, not a chore. This recipe turns it into something bright and garlicky in about the time it takes to reheat rice the boring way.

Ingredients

Serves 1-2.

  • Cooked rice, cold (day-old works best)2 cups
  • Garlic, finely chopped4 cloves
  • Butter or oil2 tbsp
  • Lemon1
  • Spring onion, chopped2 tbsp
  • Salt and black pepperto taste

Method

  1. Break up the rice. Before cooking, use your hands or a fork to break up any clumps in the cold rice — this helps it fry evenly instead of steaming in one big lump.
  2. Cook the garlic. Heat the butter in a pan over medium heat. Add the garlic and cook for about 30 seconds until fragrant, watching closely so it doesn't burn.
  3. Fry the rice. Add the rice and toss well to coat every grain in the garlic butter. Spread it out and let it sit for a minute at a time, stirring occasionally, until heated through and lightly crisp in spots — about 5 minutes.
  4. Finish. Take off the heat, squeeze the lemon over the top, and toss with spring onion, salt, and pepper. Taste and add more lemon if you want extra brightness.

Tip: Fresh, warm rice turns mushy when fried. Cold, day-old rice has dried out slightly and holds its shape far better in the pan.

Why this works

The lemon goes in at the very end, off the heat, so its acidity stays sharp instead of cooking off. Adding it too early mutes the flavor, which is the most common reason lemon rice recipes taste flat.
